Third-party services and external links

Article and advert links take you to other websites. Those sites apply their own privacy policies and may collect information or set cookies when you visit them.

Publisher images can load directly from external image hosts while you browse Reconnect. Fonts load from Google Fonts. These requests disclose technical connection information, including your IP address and browser information, to the respective providers; referrer information may also be sent according to browser rules.

Newsletter subscriptions are handled by Substack, not by Reconnect's article-click database. If you subscribe through Substack, the information you provide is processed under Substack's privacy policy. The newsletter signup and welcome-letter embeds are not currently installed on this website. This policy will need to be reviewed when they are added, including any data sent when an embed loads.

For information about Google services, see Google's privacy policy. Hosting providers may process technical information needed to serve the website; production hosting and log-retention details will be confirmed before launch.
