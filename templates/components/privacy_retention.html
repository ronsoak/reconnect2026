Retention and security

Reconnect has maintenance commands to aggregate click records into monthly totals and delete a whole month of raw clicks once that month is at least four months old, after checking that its totals have been aggregated. Monthly totals are retained indefinitely for historical article and advert performance.

Automated aggregation and deletion schedules are not yet configured. Raw click records therefore remain until maintenance is run; this policy does not promise automatic deletion after a fixed number of days. Server-log and backup retention periods have not yet been established.

Administrative data is accessed through authenticated administration tools. No online service can guarantee complete security. Production transport security, hosting access controls, maintenance schedules, and backup practices must be verified before launch.
