Information collected

Reconnect records article and advert click-throughs to understand which writing readers find useful, rank articles, prepare newsletter recaps, and measure advert performance. It also remembers your Category and Focus choices in your browser.

Requests to the website necessarily send technical information such as an IP address, requested URL, and browser information to the server. Hosting services may retain this information in access or error logs for operating the service and diagnosing problems. The production logging settings and retention period have not yet been confirmed.

Search terms and filters are sent in page URLs. They may appear in browser history and server logs; avoid entering sensitive personal information into search.

There is currently no public contact form on Reconnect. If one is added, this policy will be updated to explain the information it collects and how enquiries are handled. Information you send directly to us would be used to respond to your enquiry.
