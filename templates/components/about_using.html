Using Reconnect

The Front Page

The front page comes in three different flavours depending on your mood.

  • Dynamic: Reconnect's default view, mixes new, older, and popular articles to provide an evergreen showcase of articles. Click refresh at any point to get a new array of articles.
  • Newest: Shows the latest articles ingested into the platform, use this to see a the bleeding edge of content as it comes out.
  • Popular: Shows you trending articles on the platform, influenced by user clicks.

Filtering the feed

  • Select your chosen category to limit the feed to Articles from Sites that match your desired topic, i.e. TCGs. Please note Sites can only have one category.
  • You can further refine your results by selecting a Sites focus, i.e. Retro. A site can have multiple focuses.
  • Click Apply to action your choices, or Reset back to the default

Searching the Site

  • Enter keywords into the Search bar on any page to get a page of results for your search terms.
  • Search ignores your filters for category and focus.
  • Enjoy the silly placeholder text.

    • Article links take you to the original publication. Browse the Publications directory to find a site's own website, RSS feed, and articles.

    Subscrbing via RSS

    Reconnect is powered by the cutting edge technology of RSS, and so its only seems fit to offer that back to the users.

    Located on the Publications page, you can:

    • Subscribe to a feed of everything coming through Reconnect, no matter what the category.
    • Subscribe to a single category's feed, i.e. Video Games.
    • Subscribe to a specific Sites RSS feed by finding them in the directory.

    Simply copy a feed link from the Publications page into your preferred RSS reader. Reconnect's feeds include up to {{ rss_feed_item_limit }} of the newest articles with publication dates; individual publications will have their own limitations and format.

    Use the API

    The public, read-only API returns JSON without requiring an account. Start with these endpoints:

    • /api/feed/ for the article feed.
    • /api/search/?q=games for search results.
    • /api/filters/ for category and Focus IDs.

    Choose an ordering with ?feed=newest or ?feed=popular. Filter with category and tag IDs; repeat either parameter to select more than one. Use page for additional Newest, Popular, or search results.
