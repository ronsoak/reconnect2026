Article and advert click tracking

When a click is counted, the database records the article or advert identifier, click type (Article, RSS, or Advert), publication where applicable, and calendar date. These click records do not store your IP address, browser details, account, cookie contents, or a visitor identifier.

The browser cookie used for repeat-click protection is separate from those database records. Reconnect does not attach it to each saved click. Technical server logs are a separate source of information and may include requested click-through URLs and IP addresses.

RSS article links can record a click when followed through Reconnect. Simply fetching an RSS feed is not counted as an article click, and Reconnect does not currently estimate subscriber numbers from feed reads.

Monthly analytics retain totals per article or advert and click type, with the publication where applicable, rather than visitor profiles. Clearing a browser cookie does not remove previously recorded click totals.
