Reconnect is an aggregator of blogs from across the world, specifically about Video Games, TCGs, TTRPGs, Board Games, and adjacent geek culture.

It’s the biggest index of gaming blogs across the English-speaking internet, updates multiple times a day and is built in a way to make it easier for its users to discover new articles and writers.

It’s a site built both for the readers and the writers. For the reader it’s a place to discover something awesome to read whenever the desire strikes. For the Authors it exposes them to more readers and the community around them.
